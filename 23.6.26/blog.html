🌱 Plant Care Essentials

How to Keep Your Indoor Plants Healthy

Healthy plants require the right balance of light, water, humidity, and nutrients. Start by placing your plant in a location with suitable sunlight. Avoid overwatering and always check soil moisture before watering.

Regularly clean plant leaves, remove dead foliage, and fertilize during the growing season for stronger roots and vibrant growth.

🌼 Seasonal Planting Guide

Spring

Best season for planting flowers, herbs, and vegetable seedlings.

Summer

Focus on watering, mulching, and heat-tolerant plant varieties.

Autumn

Plant trees, shrubs, and cool-season flowers before winter arrives.

Winter

Protect sensitive plants and focus on indoor gardening projects.

🐞 Pest Control Tips

Aphid Control

Spray plants with water or use neem oil to remove aphids naturally.

Prevent Fungus Gnats

Allow soil to dry slightly between watering sessions and improve drainage.

Inspect Leaves Weekly

Early detection helps prevent pest infestations from spreading.

☀ Watering & Sunlight Instructions

Watering Tips

  • Check soil moisture first.
  • Avoid overwatering.
  • Use pots with drainage holes.
  • Reduce watering in winter.

Sunlight Tips

  • Match plants to light levels.
  • Rotate plants regularly.
  • Avoid harsh afternoon sun.
  • Use grow lights if needed.
